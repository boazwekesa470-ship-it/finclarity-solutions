QUESTIONS

What prospects usually want to know.

Is FinClarity only for accounting?

No. FinClarity connects finance with operational areas such as sales, procurement, inventory, payroll, banking, approvals and reporting.

Can one owner manage more than one business?

FinClarity supports a multi-business approach with separate business records and controlled access.

Can our existing data be migrated?

Data migration is handled as part of implementation scope. Master data and opening information are prepared and validated before go-live.

Is the system suitable for Kenyan organisations?

The product is designed with Kenyan operating needs in mind, including KES reporting, VAT controls and eTIMS readiness. Live third-party integrations depend on provider setup and approval.

How do we know which package we need?

We recommend a short discovery conversation first. Package and implementation scope are then based on your processes, users, businesses, reporting needs and integrations.
