QUESTIONS

What prospects usually want to know.

Clear answers before you commit time to a demonstration or implementation discussion.

Still have a question? Talk to us →
Is FinClarity only for accounting?+

No. FinClarity connects finance with sales, procurement, inventory, payroll, banking, approvals, reporting and administration so business activity can flow into one controlled system.

Can one owner manage more than one business?+

Yes. FinClarity supports a multi-business approach with separate business records and controlled user access, designed for owners who need clearer visibility across more than one organisation.

Can our existing Excel or system data be migrated?+

Yes, migration is handled as part of implementation scope. Master data and approved opening information are prepared, validated and loaded through controlled templates before go-live.

Is the system suitable for Kenyan organisations?+

The ERP is designed around Kenyan operational needs including KES reporting, VAT control and eTIMS readiness. Live external integrations are activated only when the required provider setup and credentials are available.

How is pricing determined?+

Pricing depends on the package, number of users and businesses, modules, migration complexity, integrations and implementation scope. We prefer to scope the business properly before giving a final commercial proposal.

Is FinClarityFX part of the ERP?+

No. FinClarityFX is a separate upcoming analytics product under the FinClarity brand. The ERP focuses on business operations and control; FinClarityFX focuses on structured market analysis and strategy validation.
